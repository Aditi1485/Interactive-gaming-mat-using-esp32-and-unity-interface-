🖼️ Results

The interactive gaming mat system was successfully implemented and tested, demonstrating accurate detection of user movements and seamless interaction with the Unity-based game environment.

When a user steps on different regions of the mat, the pressure sensors respond instantly and send signals to the ESP32 microcontroller. These signals are processed and transmitted to the Unity application, where corresponding game actions such as movement, jumping, and directional control are executed in real time.

The system exhibits high responsiveness with minimal delay, ensuring a smooth and engaging gaming experience. The accuracy and consistency of input detection make the system highly reliable and efficient.

Overall, the system successfully bridges physical interaction with digital gameplay, making it suitable for gaming, fitness training, rehabilitation, and interactive applications.
